## 🌟 Overview & Feature Parity

The Minecraft 26.1 release of **Vanilla Outsider: Better Dogs** provides complete feature parity with the 26.2 release line for servers running Minecraft `26.1` or `26.1.2`.

### Supported Feature Parity
* **Personality Engine**: Full support for Aggressive, Pacifist, and Normal wolf personality traits.
* **Scale Genetics**: Physical rendering scale attributes bounded between 70% (`bd_wolf_min_scale_percent`) and 145% (`bd_wolf_max_scale_percent`).
* **GameRules Authority**: All 60+ namespaced GameRules (`betterdogs:*`) supported under `DynamicGameRuleManager` 1.8.2.
* **Goat Horn & Boarding Systems**: Goat horn tactical commands and vehicle riding mechanics fully operational.

---

## 🔧 Installation & Requirements

1. Install **Fabric Loader** `0.19.1` or higher for Minecraft `26.1.2`.
2. Place `vanilla-outsider-better-dogs-4.24.1+26.1.2.jar` into your server or client `mods/` directory.
3. Ensure **DasikLibrary** `1.8.2` or higher is installed.
4. Launch using **JDK 25**.
